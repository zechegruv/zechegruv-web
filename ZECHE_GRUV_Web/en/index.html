The song is born in the studio.

Four sessions a month, one a week. Bring an idea, a line or just the urge: we build it together, from scratch.

  1. We start from whatever you bring, even a single line. We find the hook and shape the song: you leave the first session with a clear map.

  2. We build the beat and the sound that feels like you, with your references on the table.

  3. The takes, with all the time they need. We work on technique and emotion together.

  4. We get it ready for every platform, with your live backing tracks included.
