05 / The Label

ZECHE GRUV is an independent record label directed by Yaco Santana — over 10 years producing music, with more than 200 artists worldwide choosing us to accompany their careers.

Our goal is to keep organic sound at the core of every project, developing each production until it matches the artist's vision — professional sound and songwriting at the highest creative level. We run a full team: producers, session musicians on multiple instruments, mixing & mastering engineers, distribution across every digital platform, cover art & design, and audiovisual recording and production.
